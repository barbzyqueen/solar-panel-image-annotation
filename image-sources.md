## 3. Licensing and Redistribution

The images were obtained from publicly accessible image platforms. However, public availability does not automatically mean that an image can be redistributed without restrictions.

To avoid potential copyright and licensing issues:

- Original image files are not included in this repository.
- The source platforms are acknowledged.
- Individual image links should be provided where available.
- Anyone wishing to reproduce the project should obtain the images directly from their original sources and review the applicable licence terms.

Users are responsible for checking the licensing conditions associated with each image before downloading, using, or redistributing it.

## 4. Reproducing the Dataset

To recreate this annotation exercise:

1. Visit Unsplash or Pexels.
2. Search for photographs using terms such as:
   - Solar panels
   - Rooftop solar panels
   - Solar panel installation
   - Ground-mounted solar panels
3. Select images with clearly identifiable individual panels.
4. Download images according to the applicable platform licence.
5. Import the images into Roboflow or another compatible annotation platform.
6. Follow the annotation rules documented in [annotation-guidelines.md](annotation-guidelines.md).

The original COCO annotation export can also be found in the `annotations/` directory. However, the corresponding original images are required to reconstruct the annotations visually.
